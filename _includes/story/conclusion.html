What we can say

  • Stock: to fill in. Expected: no visible contagion stock to stock; a common shock dominates.
  • Sector: to fill in with the order and the four metrics.
  • Region: to fill in with the alignment between market and epidemic.

What we cannot say

  • Anything about causes: lead-lag is an association.
  • That our findings hold for every crisis: we compare three.
  • Exact depths for 2000 and 2008: survivorship bias, twice.
  • Fine regional conclusions: few regional ETFs, wide intervals.
  • Results free of our choices: thresholds were chosen, and we show their effect.
