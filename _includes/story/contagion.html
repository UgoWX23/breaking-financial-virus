Our verdict, to confirm with F2.3b and F2.6a

A common shock, not a chain reaction

At the scale of single stocks, we do not see the crash travelling from one stock to its neighbours. Most shocks happened on the same days for everyone, and what is left after removing the market looks no stronger inside a sector than across sectors. Contagion may exist at a faster speed than daily prices can show, but with this data it is not visible stock by stock. So we step back.
