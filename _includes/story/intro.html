What the data cannot tell

Four limits to keep in mind on every page.

Known limits

  • Survivorship bias, twice. Kaggle only holds firms listed in 2020, and Yahoo does not serve firms that have since disappeared.
  • Wrong adjusted prices. Adj Close is wrong on 0.026% of the days. We correct it, but the rule is not perfect.
  • Sector labels. They come from Yahoo, and 28% of Covid stocks have none.
  • Lead-lag is not causation. When a stock moves before another, we say it precedes it, never that it causes it.

What we do about it

  • We announce the bias under every figure about the dot-com and 2008, and build the propagation chapters on Covid only.
  • We keep the correction rule visible and tested (93.8% agreement with Yahoo on the flagged days).
  • We report the unclassified stocks instead of guessing their sector.
  • We write "precursor", never "responsible".
