Sources

Kaggle stock market datasetOne CSV per stock or ETF listed in the US, up to 1 April 2020. 8,049 files: 5,884 stocks and 2,165 ETFs. 33 files with a zero or negative Adj Close are left out.
Yahoo Finance (yfinance)History from April 2020 to 30 October 2026, with dividends, for 5,277 of the 8,049 tickers. Frozen on the download of 1 and 2 November 2026.
Sector tableYahoo labels mapped to the 11 GICS sectors, plus defensive and cyclical groups. Not official GICS labels.
Regional ETFs26 funds mapped by hand to a region. 12 usable for the dot-com, 20 for 2008, 25 for Covid.
Epidemic data (to add)Case counts by country, for example Our World in Data or Johns Hopkins.

Processing

Preparation, ticker by ticker
  • Correction of Adj Close: when the Close and Adj Close returns differ by more than 5 points, keep the one closest to zero (7,201 days, 0.026% of returns).
  • Joining Kaggle and Yahoo through returns and splits.
  • Log returns, ticker x date panel.
  • Residual returns from a market model.
  • Minimal history filter.

A ticker is excluded when it lacks history before the crisis (the market model cannot be estimated), has too many gaps during it, or trades below 1 dollar.

Known problems

  • Survivorship bias. Only firms listed in 2020 are present: the dot-com and 2008 are seen through survivors, Covid is almost complete.
  • Yahoo extension. 42% of tickers keep their Kaggle history only (delisted, merged, ticker reused by another firm, or a failed check). This affects the recovery period, not the crisis windows.
  • Adjusted prices. The correction rule can shrink a real large move on rare days, and the checks do not catch every error.
  • Sectors. 28% of the Covid stocks have no label.
